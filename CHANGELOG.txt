WoWErrands v2.0.0 (WoW Forever 1.60.1)
- Errands now registers as a native, independently collapsible module inside
  Blizzard's All Objectives, between Professions and later modules.
- Removed the separate floating WoWErrands tracker panel.
- Pending current-zone errands appear as clickable checkboxes in the module.
- Automatically refreshes on zone changes and when errands are added,
  completed or removed. The section hides when no pending errands remain.
- Collapsed state persists in existing SavedVariablesPerCharacter.
- Map-based editor, parchment theme, tabs, zone-entry checklist and saved
  data remain intact from v1.9.0.
